About Us

FOOTBALL IS THE HEARTBEAT.
TECHNOLOGY IS THE FUTURE.

XG Football Group partners with lower-division football clubs across Europe and transforms them into self-sustaining community hubs. We believe that every town, no matter how small, deserves fast internet, modern recreation, youth development programs, and a football club it can be proud of.

Our model is simple: we invest in the club, upgrade the facilities, deploy broadband and computing infrastructure, build recreation spaces like padel courts and training grounds, and create professional content that tells the stories of the people and places that make each community unique. The stadium becomes more than a pitch. It becomes the center of daily life.

The FIRM Model

FOUR PILLARS. ONE COMMUNITY.

Every deployment transforms a stadium from a venue that sits empty 340 days a year into a thriving district that serves the community every single day.

Football

Professional coaching, player development, and a culture of excellence. We rehabilitate clubs, build competitive squads, and give players the tools to grow their careers. Winning on the pitch is where community pride begins.

Infrastructure

High-speed broadband for communities that have been left behind. Edge computing that brings the power of AI to rural areas. The stadium becomes the digital hub that connects the community to the modern economy.

Recreation

Padel courts, youth academies, community training grounds, and event spaces. The stadium district becomes a place families visit daily, not just on match weekends. Active communities are healthy communities.

Media

AI-powered broadcasting brings every match to fans worldwide. We tell the stories of our players, coaches, and communities through professional content. Every club becomes a content studio, every fan becomes part of the story.

Where It Started

APEA FC:
PROOF THAT IT WORKS

In 2021, we acquired a stake in APEA FC, a distressed fourth-division club in Akrotiri, Cyprus. The club was emerging from pandemic-era insolvency with no budget, no infrastructure, and no hope of promotion. We brought professional operations, qualified coaching, modern branding, and a culture that demanded excellence. Three seasons later, the results speak for themselves.
2022 STOK Elite Division Champions. First promotion secured in inaugural season.
2023 Cyprus 3rd Division, 5th place. Professional operations established.
2024 3rd Division, 3rd place. Promoted to 2nd Division. Coca-Cola Cup Champions.
